About Us

Welcome To Sweet Deli Dan!

A homegrown Daet business, built from a small online start in 2020 into a store our community can visit.

Sweet Deli Dan was founded in 2020 by Danica Joy Asis. What began as an online business has grown steadily over six years, thanks to the trust and support of our loyal customers. In the past eight months, we opened our physical store in Daet, Camarines Norte, so that we can serve you in person.

Our Mission

To provide delicious, fresh, and affordable treats that bring happiness and satisfaction to every customer.

Our Vision

To become a trusted and well-loved food business known for quality homemade treats, excellent service, and memorable celebrations.

We are located at Governor Panotes Avenue, Barangay 8, Daet, Camarines Norte
